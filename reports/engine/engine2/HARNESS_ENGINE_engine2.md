## How to read this

* underruns / dropped are the router METERS cumulative counters (max over the run) per node id (s* source, d* destination).
* age p50/p95/p99/max are the engine lastCallbackAgeMs distributions over all METERS samples of the run.
* steady gaps are silent dropouts after signal onset (>=2 ms below -80 dBFS), i.e. real audio loss; startup gaps begin within 100 ms of onset.
* phase jumps/missing/extra come from tone phase continuity: nonzero missing/extra means the tone phase does not advance with elapsed time, i.e. skipped/duplicated samples (not just silence).
* spurious recurrence events away from known gaps are classified (frozen thresholds): a REAL splice needs local deviation > max(8*sigma_residual, 4e-3) AND an adjacent-sample delta outlier vs the expected tone derivative (delta > 1.5*amp*w); any other event is a SUB-SAMPLE resampler-trim artifact. PASS requires zero real splices; sub-sample artifacts are warnings only.
* PASS/FAIL is per capture: silent dropouts, held samples, phase discontinuities, real splices, tone errors or capture shortfall all fail.
